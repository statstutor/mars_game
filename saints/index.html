✝ Saints Memory ✝

Match each saint with the words they spoke

How to play

  • Flip two cards at a time. A saint's picture matches the card with their quote.
  • The game starts with 3 pairs. Each time someone wins, the next game gets one more pair.
  • Single player: every card you flip uses one flip. Match them all before the flips run out.
  • A green ✓ means you found a match. A red ✗ means the cards don't match; they stay face-up until you tap again.
  • Two players: make a match and you go again; miss and it's the other player's turn. Whoever has the most matches wins the round and gets 1 point. Play as many rounds as you like.
